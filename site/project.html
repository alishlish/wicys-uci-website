HOW IT WORKS

Every quarter, WiCyS gives all participants one realistic cybersecurity challenge. Teams of 3–5 build their own technical solution to that problem over 8 weeks. Through workshops and mentorship, teams compete to solve the brief and demo their project at Demo Day to industry judges.
